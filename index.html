Projects

MoTE-VLM: a vision-language model on a Raspberry Pi cluster

I replace the feed-forward layers of a transformer with small experts and place one expert on each Raspberry Pi. The latest version is a 206M-parameter model that runs entirely on four Pi 5 boards with no GPU, and captions COCO images at 70.4 CIDEr.

Qwen2.5-VL for nuclear decommissioning images

Inspection images from the Fukushima Daiichi decommissioning site are often dark and blurry. I asked whether a vision-language model could describe them on small edge hardware. I quantized Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct to 8 bits with BitsAndBytes and proposed a three-part design that would spread the work across a Raspberry Pi cluster. This was my first project and the starting point for MoTE-VLM.

Supported by JAEA Nuclear Energy S&T and Human Resource Development Project, Grant JPJA23O23813888. Test images: Tokyo Electric Power Company Holdings.
