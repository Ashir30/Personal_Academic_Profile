Research Profile & Focus

I am an award-winning MS researcher working at the intersection of efficient machine learning systems and real-world distributed AI deployment. My research focuses on making multi-billion parameter Vision-Language Models (VLMs) and Large Language Models (LLMs) execute efficiently on resource-constrained commodity edge hardware.

My framework, MoTE-VLM (Mixture of Tiny Experts for Vision-Language Models), deployed across a 3-node Raspberry Pi 5 cluster, won the Best Presentation Award at ICCAI 2026 (ACM) and was rated “Highly Innovative” during peer review.

Currently, I am completing my master's research funded by the MEXT (Japanese Government) Scholarship at Iwate Prefectural University under the supervision of Prof. Hirokazu Madokoro and Prof. Stephanie Nix.

Efficient Machine Learning Systems

Model compression, INT4/INT8 quantization, load-balancing loss formulation, and sparse computation.

Distributed Edge AI & SBC Clusters

Distributed pipeline inference across single-board computers (Raspberry Pi 5 clusters) and embedded GPU/edge accelerators.

Vision-Language Models (VLMs)

Multimodal architectures (Qwen-VL, Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct), cross-modal routing, and vision-token compression.

Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) Architectures

Top-1 gating functions, dynamic expert selection under distributional shift, and minimal routing overhead algorithms.
