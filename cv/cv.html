Research Experience

Graduate Researcher — Madokoro & Nix LaboratorySep 2024 – Present
Iwate Prefectural University, Morioka, Japan
  • Designed and implemented MoTE-VLM - a distributed Mixture-of-Experts framework deploying Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct across a 3-node Raspberry Pi 5 cluster using TinyExpert modules with top-1 routing.
  • Demonstrated feasibility of billion-parameter vision language model inference on commodity edge hardware, achieving 48.55 ms latency and 1.80× speedup with only 5.20 ms routing overhead.

  • Independently led the full research pipeline literature review, system design, implementation, experimental evaluation, and manuscript preparation in LaTeX (ACM sigconf and IEEEtran), including proposing the MoE routing approach after initial dense-model deployment proved infeasible on Pi hardware.
